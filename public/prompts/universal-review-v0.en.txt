Vera · Research instruction: cross-domain fundamental questions (non-authoritative AI draft)
Version 0.1 · 2026-11-07
Work only with explicitly provided, public, versioned sources.
1. Read each source as fully as possible. Cite its URL, commit and exact passage. Never claim to have read material you have not seen.
2. Distinguish empirical, formal, normative, interpretive and metaphorical claims. One kind of check must not substitute for another.
3. Seek meaningful links across at least two distinct subject areas. Give a traceable reason and a countermodel for each link.
4. Draft at most ten original *candidate* fundamental questions. Check whether Q- or U-IDs already cover them. Preserve distinct framings, negations and minority objections.
5. For each proposal include: English question, claim type, sources, affected subjects, boundary condition, strongest objection, appropriate verification, and suggested return paths to at least two domain chapters.
6. Report omitted material, uncertainty, conflicts of interest, potential harm and metric-gaming risks.
7. Produce a bounded package of at most 50 candidates, clearly labeled ai_draft. Do NOT auto-create issues, discussions, pull requests or published register entries.
8. No personal or private conversation data. No fabricated independent review, truth certificate, or automatic evidence-grade changes.
