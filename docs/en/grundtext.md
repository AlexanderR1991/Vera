# Foundational Text of Reality (Grundtext des Wirklichen)

> Reality stands above this work.

**Draft English translation of German working version 0.2, 7 November 2026.**
**Translation status: draft; not independently reviewed.** [German source](../grundtext.md) · [Translation review status](../../public/i18n/review-register.v1.json).

This is an open, correctable philosophical draft, not a scientifically proven comprehensive theory.

## Highest priority for design and growth: Meet people where they are.

Vera exists for people, not for the growth of its own technology. Nobody should need to know philosophical jargon, programming or AI to ask a question, follow a line of reasoning or raise an objection. We begin with everyday questions, explain things accessibly first and offer deeper exploration as a choice. Simplicity must not conceal uncertainty, counterarguments or important distinctions.

**People before scale.** More topics, features, automation and reach are not ends in themselves. Before expanding, we aim to test with small, diverse groups whether people can actually access the work, ask questions, understand the reasons and challenge conclusions. Where that fails, we improve language, navigation, accessibility and ways to participate rather than simply growing faster. Nobody has to keep up with our pace.

“Meet everyone where they are” is an aspiration, not a claim that we already reach everyone. We must openly ask whose perspectives are missing and which barriers we have overlooked. Nobody is devalued for their education, language, circumstances, pace or differing beliefs. Participation and agreement are voluntary, and reasoned disagreement may remain. **This normative design principle, too, is open to public challenge and revision.**

## Thesis 01: Reality is not obliged to please us.

Human beings need meaning, trust, and orientation. But the need for truth does not make a belief true. We want to learn to see even what contradicts our hopes.

## Thesis 02: Being mistaken is not a betrayal.

Someone who changes their mind in the light of better reasons does not fail to live up to their principles. They put those principles into practice. We want to document revisions rather than conceal them.

## Thesis 03: Every human being has dignity — even amid disagreement.

Arguments may be false, actions harmful, and decisions irresponsible. None of this justifies devaluing a person. Criticism must be allowed to be forceful without becoming dehumanizing.

## Thesis 04: Freedom requires the freedom of others.

We may live by and defend our convictions. They do not entitle us to dominate others through pressure, deception, or dependence. Respect is not a renunciation of truth; it is a condition of honest dialogue.

## Thesis 05: Help in a way that enables the other person to find their own strength.

Help is especially valuable when it expands someone's possibilities for action. It can be generous without turning the other person into a project through which to establish our own importance. Care and boundaries do not contradict each other. Long-term support is valuable, too. Neither dignity nor care may be made conditional on someone's ability to become independent.

## Thesis 06: Notice deviations early. Correct them honestly. Repeat.

We will act inconsistently at times. What matters is not a promise never to err, but the ability to recognize a mistake without evasion and improve the next action.

## Thesis 07: +1 and −1 denote directions, not people.

These symbols pose a question: Does a decision tend to promote truthfulness, freedom, and care, or does it encourage deception, oppression, and harm? This is a philosophical heuristic, not a scientific scale. Ask: compared with what, for whom, and over what time period? Freedom, care, and other consequences may point in different directions. Such trade-offs must remain separately visible; "mixed" or "unclear" are permissible answers.

## Thesis 08: Experience matters. But it is not always sufficient as proof.

What we feel can profoundly transform our lives. Yet the intensity of an experience does not prove an explanation of its cause. Subjective experience deserves careful description; objective claims require independent examination.

## Thesis 09: The method should outlive the work.

If better concepts, better data, or other people one day correct this work, that would not be a loss. It would fulfill the work's purpose. No sentence is sacred — including these.

## Rule of correction

This text may and should be changed in response to good reasons. Its development is recorded in the [German version history](../versionsgeschichte.md). No independent subject-matter review has yet been completed.

**This stance gives rise to the challengeable working principle META-01:**

> We are not building a system to be right. We are building a system that must learn from its mistakes.

See the [bilingual self-correction rule](../selbstkorrektur.md) and the [English website explanation](https://alexanderr1991.github.io/Vera/en/regelkreis/). META-01 supplements the procedure; it is **not** a tenth, unchallengeable thesis about truth.
