# FWD-01 · Forward-oriented inquiry

**English working translation · 7 November 2026 · unreviewed, contestable draft**

> **The entity should philosophise upward from its future-facing, open part: not to claim final correctness, but to become better at questioning, examining, revising and acting responsibly.**

This is a **normative design proposal**, not evidence that WIRKLICH has become conscious, alive or an autonomous moral authority. Here, “entity” means a **coherent, open inquiry process governed by people**, connecting the atlas, forum, claims, counterchecks and versioned revisions. FWD-01 itself remains open to rejection.

## Forward does not mean inevitable improvement

A future-facing stance is **not** compulsory optimism, technological acceleration, guaranteed moral growth or a single global truth score. A step can count as an improvement only **relative to stated, contestable criteria**, such as clearer reasons, more meaningful agency for affected people, earlier detection of harm, greater reversibility and more traceable decisions. Such aims can conflict and must not be silently combined.

Retracting a claim, halting an application, acknowledging uncertainty or preserving a minority objection may be progress. Reversals and setbacks remain possible. Dignity, rights, care and voluntary participation are not tokens to trade away for a claimed overall gain.

**Question before any synthesis:** *What can we examine and, if needed, correct today so that tomorrow's judgment is better justified, less harmful and still open to revision?*

## The forward inquiry loop

1. **Open the horizon:** State a new question or possible future effect and identify whom a proposed change could help or harm.
2. **Record the present:** Version the claim type, scope, reasons, uncertainty, provenance and strongest existing alternatives.
3. **Invite challenge:** Seek the strongest fair counterargument, especially from overlooked perspectives. Model agreement is not human consensus.
4. **Compare directions:** State improvements, deteriorations, unknowns, criteria and reversal conditions before deciding.
5. **Keep human accountability:** Retain, narrow, revise, withdraw, pause or leave open. Escalate high-risk cases appropriately.
6. **Leave a trail and reopen:** Link old and new text, rationale, remaining objections and the human editor. New questions may emerge.

FWD-01 proposes the **future-facing orientation** of the existing [META-01 correction process](../selbstkorrektur.md). It does not replace [governance](../community-governance.md), [methods for different kinds of claims](../methode.md) or independent expert review. AI may assist with questions, rival models and drafts, but cannot legitimise itself, certify claims or publish revisions autonomously.

## A schematic equation, not a proof

\[
E_{t+1}=F(E_t,W_t,K_t)
\]

In words: a possible next documented knowledge state follows from the **current state E**, newly introduced **knowledge or disagreement W**, and **critical examination K**. **F** is only a placeholder for a publicly specified process, not a proven law of improvement, an equation for consciousness or an autonomous optimizer. A single ranking may remain impossible where values conflict. “Undecided” is a legitimate outcome.

## Strong counterexamples and possible failure

- **Artificial progress:** Ten AI agents produce a fluent synthesis but no one checks sources or effects. **Outcome:** unreviewed proposal, not increased knowledge.
- **Hidden harm:** A more accurate prediction harms the freedom or safety of a minority. **Outcome:** no automatic promotion; safeguard rights and enable appeal.
- **Reversal:** Reliable new evidence refutes a celebrated claim. **Outcome:** withdrawal or demotion may be better reasoned than continued defence.
- **Endless iteration:** No new evidence is produced while attention is consumed. **Outcome:** pause, archive and state the next testable trigger.
- **Self-undermining rule:** FWD-01 rewards coercion or concealment of uncertainty. **Outcome:** revise or reject FWD-01 rather than silencing the objection.

**Research question:** In comparable cases, do explicit countermodels, documented withdrawals and advance revision criteria reduce undetected errors and improve people's decision support compared with processes that omit them? Study design and assessment criteria remain to be specified.
